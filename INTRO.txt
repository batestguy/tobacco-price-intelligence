4.  FEATURE ENGINEERING & ML MODEL (Forecasting)
================================================================================

TARGET: Predict weekly demand per SKU per region for next 4 weeks.

FEATURES TO GENERATE:
  - Lagged FX rates (t-1, t-7, t-30)
  - Inflation rate (monthly)
  - Competitor price index (weighted average)
  - News crisis score (t-1, t-3)

  - Tax change dummy
  - Holiday dummies (Sallah, Christmas, New Year)
  - Region dummies (Lagos, Ibadan, Kano, PH)

MODEL OPTIONS (All Free):
  A) XGBoost – robust, handles nonlinearities, missing values
  B) LightGBM – faster than XGBoost, similar accuracy
  C) Facebook Prophet – specifically designed for time series with holidays
  D) Scikit-learn RandomForest – simple, good for baseline

RECOMMENDED: XGBoost (train on Google Colab free GPU/CPU)

TRAINING STRATEGY:
  - Initial training: Use 3+ years of synthetic or historical data (if provided)
  - Retraining: Weekly via GitHub Actions (or manually)
  - Online Learning: Use incremental updates with XGBoost (xgb.train with 
    'process_type': 'update')

EVALUATION METRICS:
  - RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
  - MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)

5.  OPTIMIZATION ENGINE (Pricing & Inventory Rebalancing)
================================================================================

INPUT: Forecasted demand, current stock levels, FX rate, competitor prices.
OUTPUT: Recommended price increase (%) and inventory transfer suggestions.

FREE TOOL OPTIONS:
  A) SciPy (linprog) – linear programming, easy to use
  B) PuLP – more flexible LP/MILP solver
  C) Google OR-Tools – supports linear and integer programming
  D) Pyomo – comprehensive optimization framework

RECOMMENDED: SciPy linprog (lightweight, no external dependencies)

CONSTRAINTS TO IMPLEMENT:
  - Price cannot exceed competitor average + 5%
  - Price cannot be lower than cost + 10% margin
  - Inventory must stay between safety stock and max capacity
  - Total rebalancing cost must be minimized

OBJECTIVE: Maximize total profit = Σ(Price * Demand) - Σ(Inventory Holding Cost)
